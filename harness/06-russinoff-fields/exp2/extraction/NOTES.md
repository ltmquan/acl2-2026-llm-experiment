## Targets (targets.lisp)
| Target | Authors' name(s) | Source | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| MAIN-1 | `field-axioms-p-fieldp` | extensions.lisp:2585 | every `fieldp` object satisfies all 16 field axioms (`field-axioms-p`) |
| MAIN-2 | `len-proots-bound` ∧ `member-proots` | polynomials.lisp:484, :502 | a non-constant monic p over f has at most deg p roots; `(proots p f)` lists exactly its roots |
| MAIN-3 | `prootp-min-poly`, neutral form | polynomials.lisp:630 | every x in an extension e of f satisfies `(algebraic-witness x e f)`: x is a root of (the lift of) a monic irreducible p over f with 1 <= deg p <= [e:f] |
| MAIN-4 | `all-embeddings` ∧ `len-embeddings`, neutral form | embeddings.lisp:644, :653 | every duplicate-free list `l` of embeddings of e in k over f (`dlistp`, `all-embeddingp`) has length <= [e:f] (`edegree`) |

MAIN-2 conjoins the two authors' theorems, which have the same hypotheses. MAIN-3 and MAIN-4 use the neutral
forms that the survey proposed (`main-theorems/russinoff-fields.md`). `min-poly`, `zero-poly`, the
row-reduction code, `embeddings`, `consl` and `simple-embedding(s)-extensions` are therefore NOT in defs.lisp.

## Deviations from the authors' text
1. **The termination hint of `factorization` is changed.** The authors write
   `:hints (("Goal" :use (reduciblep-product)))`. `reduciblep-product` (polynomials.lisp:281) states
   `(pmul d q f) = p` and similar facts, which needs most of the MAIN-1 theory. Proving it, even locally,
   would put the route to MAIN-1 inside defs.lisp. Termination only needs `len(pfactor) < len(p)` (immediate
   from `reduciblep`) and `len(pquot p d f) < len(p)`. The second is a purely syntactic bound on `pdivide`'s
   quotient: len <= max(1, len x - 1) for non-constant y. We prove it locally. The hint is now
   `:hints (("Goal" :in-theory (enable reduciblep)))`. The measure `(len p)` and the body are unchanged.
2. The package is DM, with `cert.acl2`, as described above.
3. Only the definitions that the targets need are included. `fbase`, and the redundant second copy of
   `pdivides` (extensions.lisp:2239), are omitted.
4. Targets: the authors' `defthmd` became `(defthm MAIN-k ...)` with no hints. MAIN-2 is a conjunction.
   MAIN-3 and MAIN-4 are neutral restatements (see above). They are proved FROM the authors' theorems in V3.
5. Library difference (not in defs.lisp): master's `projects/linear/field` adds guards and a `defattach` to
   the constrained `fp f+ …`. The 8.7 version has neither. The constraints are the same, and
   `primep`/`ninit`/`dlistp` have identical bodies in both (see V3).
